Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Surprise Vista Solutions (“we”, “our”, or “us”) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our websites, products, platforms, applications, and communication services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Business and event-related information shared by users
  • Messages and communication submitted through WhatsApp, forms, or chat systems
  • Usage data such as browser type, IP address, and device information
  • Order, enquiry, and customer support information

2. WhatsApp and AI Communication

Our platforms may use WhatsApp Business APIs and AI-powered assistants to provide customer support, product assistance, event planning guidance, and automated responses.

By communicating with us through WhatsApp or AI chat systems, you consent to processing of messages for support, automation, analytics, and service improvement purposes.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our products and services
  • Respond to customer enquiries and support requests
  • Process orders and gifting requests
  • Improve AI and automation systems
  • Send updates, confirmations, and service notifications
  • Maintain platform security and prevent misuse

4. Data Protection

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, or loss.

However, no online system can guarantee absolute security. Users are advised not to share highly sensitive personal information through public communication channels.

5. Third-Party Services

Our services may integrate with trusted third-party platforms including cloud hosting providers, payment gateways, analytics providers, AI systems, and messaging services such as WhatsApp Business APIs.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our websites may use cookies and analytics tools to understand visitor behavior and improve user experience.

7. User Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information where legally applicable
  • Opt out of promotional communications

8. Children's Privacy

Some of our educational platforms and child-focused services may be designed for supervised usage by parents, schools, or guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate supervision or consent.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:

Surprise Vista Solutions
Website: https://surprisevista.com
Email: info@surprisevista.com
